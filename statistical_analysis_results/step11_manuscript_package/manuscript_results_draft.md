## Statistical robustness results

Shapiro-Wilk tests were available for 21 configuration-outcome combinations; normality was rejected at the 5% level in 21 of them. Q-Q plots, skewness, and excess kurtosis were also examined, so the normality test was used as a diagnostic rather than as the sole basis for test selection. Tukey's 1.5×IQR rule was applied to all 21 configuration-outcome combinations. It identified 161 outlying observations, with at least one outlier occurring in 20 of the 21 combinations. These observations were retained in the principal analysis; the outlier analysis is descriptive rather than a deletion rule.

For the Moderate profile, significant Kruskal-Wallis differences across L were observed for Time (p=<0.001, epsilon-squared=0.608); Quality (p=<0.001, epsilon-squared=0.818). Significant Holm-adjusted L-scaling pairwise differences were found for: Time (Slow profile, L=32 vs L=43, adjusted p=<0.001); Quality (Slow profile, L=32 vs L=43, adjusted p=<0.001); Time (Moderate profile, L=32 vs L=62, adjusted p=<0.001); Time (Moderate profile, L=32 vs L=160, adjusted p=<0.001); Time (Moderate profile, L=62 vs L=160, adjusted p=<0.001); Quality (Moderate profile, L=32 vs L=62, adjusted p=<0.001); Quality (Moderate profile, L=32 vs L=160, adjusted p=<0.001); Quality (Moderate profile, L=62 vs L=160, adjusted p=<0.001); Time (Fast profile, L=32 vs L=62, adjusted p=<0.001); Subtours (Fast profile, L=32 vs L=62, adjusted p=0.011); Quality (Fast profile, L=32 vs L=62, adjusted p=<0.001).

## Parameter-sensitivity results

Parameter sensitivity was examined for the Moderate profile with L=62, using the same candidate instances under every parameter setting. The repetition limit was varied over r=1,2,3 with s=3, while the pause limit was varied over s=3,4,5 with r=1. Shapiro-Wilk tests were available for 15 configuration-outcome combinations; normality was rejected at the 5% level in 15 of them. Q-Q plots, skewness, and excess kurtosis were also examined, so the normality test was used as a diagnostic rather than as the sole basis for test selection. Tukey's 1.5×IQR rule was applied to all 15 configuration-outcome combinations. It identified 149 outlying observations, with at least one outlier occurring in 13 of the 15 combinations. These observations were retained in the principal analysis; the outlier analysis is descriptive rather than a deletion rule.

Significant paired omnibus effects were observed for r sensitivity (s=3) / Time (p=<0.001, Kendall's W=0.080); r sensitivity (s=3) / Quality (p=<0.001, Kendall's W=0.990); s sensitivity (r=1) / Quality (p=<0.001, Kendall's W=0.348). Significant Holm-adjusted r/s pairwise differences were found for: Time (r=1,s=3 vs r=2,s=3, adjusted p=0.039); Time (r=1,s=3 vs r=3,s=3, adjusted p=0.005); Quality (r=1,s=3 vs r=2,s=3, adjusted p=<0.001); Quality (r=1,s=3 vs r=3,s=3, adjusted p=<0.001); Quality (r=2,s=3 vs r=3,s=3, adjusted p=<0.001); Quality (r=1,s=3 vs r=1,s=4, adjusted p=<0.001); Quality (r=1,s=3 vs r=1,s=5, adjusted p=<0.001); Quality (r=1,s=4 vs r=1,s=5, adjusted p=<0.001).
